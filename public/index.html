A small adventure in four places

Lantern Night

Tonight the whole village lights its lanterns at once, and you promised to bring them. Walk the village, talk to your neighbors, and get the lanterns lit.

  • ← → or A D to walk, or click where you want to go
  • E or Space to talk, use things and go through doors
  • Space or click to read on; number keys pick answers

There are no right or wrong choices. What you do changes the village, and the village can always recover. About 15 minutes, with sound.

A game about one skill from DBT (dialectical behavior therapy). It is not therapy and not a crisis resource. If you are in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, contact local emergency services (in the US, call or text 988).
